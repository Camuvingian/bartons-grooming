# Barton's: The Good-Hair Club

The direction is an independent illustration-led grooming studio. The headline “Less shaggy. More swagger.” balances a confident editorial layout with an affectionate, scruffy dog. The mascot is original SVG artwork shared by the logo and hero, with a simplified favicon. Blinks, a small ear twitch, a wagging tail and a pencil underline bring it to life; reduced-motion preferences stop the animations.

## Three wardrobes

- **Sketchbook (default):** warm textured paper, graphite outlines, red-pencil emphasis, imperfect card corners and a handwritten-feeling serif annotation. A sketchbook, rather than a school exercise book: generous space and clean body typography keep it sophisticated.
- **Risograph:** apricot paper, cobalt ink, lemon accents, halftone illustration backdrops, offset shadows and square print-like cards. Inspired by independent zines and small print studios.
- **After Hours:** midnight green, acid yellow, lilac ears and oversized uppercase lettering. A fashion-club personality with a softly luminous orbital frame.

The hero exposes all three directions. Existing colour palettes, lettering options and light/dark settings remain available in settings. Preferences persist through the existing provider; first-paint defaults match the new Sketchbook theme.

## Implementation scope

New SVG mascot, wordmark, favicon, editorial hero, theme controls and theme-dependent card treatments. Existing services, estimator, gallery and booking flow retain their logic. No extra runtime dependencies.

## Content still requiring owner input

The existing site contains placeholder contact details, identical before/after images and a local demonstration booking flow. Real contact details, paired grooming photography and a live booking service are separate from this visual redesign. The new hero avoids the old unverified scarcity and five-star claims.
